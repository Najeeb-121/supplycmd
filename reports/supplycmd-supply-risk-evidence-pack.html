Executive conclusion

This evidence pack supplies four official historical precedents. It does not invent confidential ERP facts. Public authorities and company filings can establish a disruption, its dates, an affected commodity, or a recall scope; they cannot establish the inventory, vendor contract, bill of materials, purchase orders, or approved alternatives of the SupplyCMD tenant.

Correct demonstration: load a genuine, timestamped Odoo baseline; map the official event to a tenant-owned product and supplier; run a supported SupplyCMD scenario without changing Odoo; retain the structured inputs and deterministic result.
Prohibited claim: these public records alone do not prove the financial or stockout impact on the Pepsico acceptance company. That consequence must be calculated from its verified Odoo baseline.

Interpretation rules

  • Each scenario is independent and starts from the same frozen baseline.
  • Do not add impacts across scenarios unless SupplyCMD later supports and validates compound scenarios.
  • Market price is not vendor price; recalled scope is not observed contamination rate; facility disruption is not proof of supplier exclusivity.
  • Unsupported values remain null, UNKNOWN, N/A, or explicitly unsupported.

Supply Risk 1 — Supplier delay

Historical evidence: Ever Given and the Suez Canal

Data pointVerified valueAuthority
Grounding date23 March 2021International Maritime Organization
Successful refloating29 March 2021Suez Canal Authority, indexed in the IMO Maritime Knowledge Centre
Canal navigation statusNavigation resumed in both directions and the canal operated around the clockSuez Canal Authority, 31 March 2021
Official characterization of recovery durationRecovery achieved “within 6 days”Suez Canal Authority, 30 May 2021

SupplyCMD parameter

scenarioType = SUPPLIER_DELAY
productId    = [tenant-owned Odoo-mapped product]
supplierId   = [tenant-owned supplier whose actual route used Suez]
delayDays    = 6

Mapping condition: six days is defensible only when the selected shipment or supplier lane depended on the Suez transit during the closure. It is not a universal six-day delay for every supplier. Queue clearance, origin/destination and downstream congestion could change an individual shipment’s realized delay.

Required real Odoo records

  • Supplier country and documented transport route or shipment record.
  • Material/product and its genuine supplier relationship.
  • Inventory at the information-cutoff date.
  • Confirmed purchase orders and delivery dates.
  • BoMs connecting the delayed component to finished products.
  • Supported demand during the six-day shift.

Analysis expected from SupplyCMD

Shift the affected confirmed receipt by six days, then recalculate the deterministic consequence supported by the engine: inventory availability, exposed downstream products, and structured shortage/stockout consequences. Do not assume lost sales or financial loss unless the existing formulas and data support them.

Supply Risk 2 — Supplier quality failure

Historical evidence: 2021 onion recall

FDA traceback identified ProSource Produce LLC and Keeler Family Farms as suppliers of potentially contaminated whole fresh onions imported from Chihuahua, Mexico. ProSource recalled red, yellow and white onions imported from 1 July through 31 August 2021. FDA listed downstream companies that processed or repackaged the onions.

Named supplierProSource Produce LLC (also known as ProSource Inc.)FDA
MaterialWhole fresh red, white and yellow onions from Chihuahua, MexicoFDA/CDC
ProSource import-date scope1 July–31 August 2021FDA final investigation page
Final reported outbreak outcome1,040 illnesses; 260 hospitalizations; 0 deathsFDA/CDC
Operational directionDo not eat, sell or serve recalled productCDC

SupplyCMD parameter

scenarioType = SUPPLIER_QUALITY_FAILURE
productId    = [tenant product proven to be within recalled scope]
supplierId   = [tenant supplier mapped to ProSource or applicable source]
failurePct   = 100
Meaning of 100%: this is a conservative operational unusability assumption for the specifically recalled inventory, because authorities directed that it not be sold or served. It is not a claim that laboratory testing found 100% of onions contaminated.

If the Odoo lot cannot be connected to the named supplier, origin and date window, this scenario must be rejected. For a beverage-sector demonstration, replace this event with an officially documented recall involving a genuine beverage ingredient supplier in the participating company’s Odoo data.

Required real Odoo records

  • Supplier identity and product relationship.
  • Incoming lot/batch, origin, receipt date and quantity.
  • Remaining available and quarantined quantities.
  • BoMs and downstream products consuming the material.
  • Alternative approved suppliers only if genuinely recorded.

Supply Risk 3 — Single-source failure

Historical evidence: Renesas Naka factory and Toyota, 2011

The Great East Japan Earthquake forced five Renesas wafer fabs and three assembly/test sites to suspend production. Renesas identified Naka as one of its worst-affected factories and stated that it produced just under 20% of its MCUs and SoC solutions and just under 10% of its analog and power devices. Finished and work-in-process inventory was expected to cover about 70% of Naka customer orders due by the end of May, leaving 30% to be addressed through production shifts and other measures.

Material limitation: the official records establish a severe bottleneck and facility outage, but do not prove that a specific Toyota part was contractually single-sourced from Renesas. Supplier exclusivity must be established by the tenant’s Odoo supplier-product records. Do not enter Renesas as a beverage supplier or claim the Toyota loss as SupplyCMD’s predicted result.

Required real Odoo records

  • A product with exactly one verified active and qualified supplier.
  • No hidden or inactive alternative that remains operationally approved.
  • Inventory, confirmed inbound, BoM dependencies and supported demand.
  • Any alternative comparison must use real vendor records, currencies, UoMs and effective dates.

Verified calculations

Increase per tonne = 3,498.373043478261 − 2,003.9755
                   = 1,494.397543478261 USD/tonne

Shock percentage   = 1,494.397543478261 ÷ 2,003.9755 × 100

                   = 3.498373043478261 USD/kg

For presentation-only conversion using 0.709 JOD/USD, March 2022 equals approximately 2.48034649 JOD/kg. The Central Bank of Jordan publishes a USD range in fils; a report must identify the chosen rate and date rather than calling a rounded 0.71 conversion exact.

scenarioType = SUPPLIER_PRICE_SHOCK
productId    = [tenant aluminium product]

shockPct     = 74.5716473818298
Market proxy: this parameter reproduces the World Bank market movement. It does not prove an identical increase in a supplier contract. Hedging, regional premiums, fabrication, freight, currency and contract dates may produce a different vendor-specific change.

Required real Odoo records

  • Actual aluminium packaging/material product and supplier relationship.
  • Vendor price, original currency, purchase UoM and effective date.
  • BoM usage per finished-good unit.
  • Supported procurement or production quantity.
  • Odoo currency rate or a separately cited conversion policy.

Execution and acceptance protocol

Pre-run gates

  1. Freeze and identify the Odoo baseline and application commit.
  2. Verify tenant ownership for every product, supplier and production record.
  3. Verify product variant/template, currency and UoM mappings.
  4. Verify the external event applies to the selected supplier/product relationship.
  5. Reject missing, impossible, unsupported or non-finite parameters.
  6. Record all unsupported facts before execution.

Run design

Run four independent simulations against the same baseline. A scenario may read ERP truth but must not mutate Odoo. Repeat each run and compare the structured response byte-for-byte where timestamps or generated IDs do not legitimately vary.

Acceptance matrix

DimensionPass condition

Result classification

  • Validated: evidence, mapping and calculation all pass.
  • Validated with limitations: result is valid under an explicitly bounded market or quarantine assumption.
  • Inconclusive: public evidence or ERP mapping is insufficient.
  • Rejected: scenario parameter or mapping is unsupported.
  • Failed: deterministic result cannot be reproduced or tenant safety fails.

Recommended headline

SupplyCMD converts verified external disruptions into tenant-safe, deterministic consequences using the manufacturer’s own Odoo baseline—and shows where every material number came from.

Do not claim

  • That SupplyCMD would have “solved” a historical crisis.
  • That the Toyota loss is a predicted loss for a beverage manufacturer.
  • That every recalled unit was laboratory-confirmed contaminated.
  • That LME/World Bank market movement equals a vendor invoice increase.
  • That every shipment through Suez was delayed by exactly six days.

Data still required from the manufacturer

No public source can truthfully fill these values: current inventory, reservations, confirmed inbound supply, vendor quotation, contractual lead time, approved alternatives, product-level demand, BoM consumption, routing, work-centre capacity and company-specific financial exposure. The PDF is therefore a complete external-evidence pack and ERP data specification—not a fabricated Odoo dataset.

Evidence-quality note

Official sources sometimes report approximate values, evolving case counts or forecasts. This report preserves those qualifiers. The chosen SupplyCMD parameters are not universal truths: each becomes valid only after the specified Odoo mapping and decision-date checks pass.
